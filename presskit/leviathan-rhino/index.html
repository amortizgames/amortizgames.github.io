Leviathan: Rhino

The final days of an old man in a small village while a giant Leviathan draws near. An existential horror about inevitability, attachment, loneliness and guilt.

View on Steam Watch trailer Assets More Games

The game

There are things beyond our control. Changes that cannot be resisted. Sometimes, all we can do is leave, or stay and watch everything we loved disappear.

In this world, leviathans, colossal creatures of Lovecraftian scale, have long since become part of everyday life. They cannot be stopped. Humanity can only track their movements and get out of their way in time. But for some, there is nowhere left to go and no reason to leave.

Live through the final days of an old man in a small village while a giant Leviathan draws near. Explore the village from a first-person perspective, talk to its residents, and carry out simple everyday tasks.

Feed the cow. Visit your neighbor. Listen to the news. Remember the past. Wait.

  • Six days, one village, one story
  • Hand-drawn characters in a 3D world
  • Single-player, no voice acting: the story is told in text, in 12 languages

Leviathan: Rhino is an existential horror game about inevitability, attachment, loneliness, guilt, and the fragile hope that even at the very end, there may still be light.

Content note: the story deals with death, grief and suicide, and includes horror imagery and some violence.
