Leviathan: Rhino

Support

For iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Every message is read and answered by a person. Write to amortizgames@gmail.com in English or Russian, and please include your device model and iOS version.

Controls

On iPhone and iPad you play by touch: an on-screen stick to move, taps to use objects, and a fire button. You can also use an MFi, Xbox, or PlayStation controller; when a controller is in use the on-screen controls hide, and touching the screen brings them back.

Saves

The game saves automatically as you play. Choose Continue on the main menu to resume from your last checkpoint. Progress is stored on your device only; deleting the app deletes it, and progress does not move between devices on its own.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

The first days of the game are free. Going further is unlocked by a single one-time purchase, the Full Game Unlock. There is nothing else to buy, and the unlock is permanent.

If you already bought it, or the game still looks locked after paying:

  • Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase and is online.
  • Open the unlock screen: it appears when the game stops you at the end of the free part, and afterwards as Unlock full game on the main menu.
  • Tap Restore purchase instead of buying again. The game confirms on screen when the unlock arrives.

If Restore purchase reports that nothing was found, the purchase was most likely made on a different Apple Account. Apple handles all billing; you can look up a charge at reportaproblem.apple.com. If that does not resolve it, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Reporting a bug

  • Your device and iOS / iPadOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • Where it happened in the game, and what you were doing
  • What happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can
