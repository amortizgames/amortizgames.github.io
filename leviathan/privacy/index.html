Leviathan: Rhino

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Leviathan: Rhino (“the App”) collects, uses, and discloses when you use it on iOS and iPadOS. The App is published by Sergei Alekseev, an individual (“we,” “us,” or “the publisher”). The game is developed by AmortizGames.

Leviathan: Rhino does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to identify you.

What information stays on your device

The App saves your game progress, in-game settings, and — if you buy the full game — a record that the purchase was made. This information is stored in the App's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and we never receive a copy of it.

If you delete the App, this information is deleted along with it. If your device backs up app data (for example, via iCloud or a computer backup), that backup is controlled entirely by your own device settings, not by us.

In-app purchases

The App offers one optional purchase: a one-time “Full Game Unlock” that permanently unlocks the rest of the game. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's In-App Purchase system. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account; Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

For information about how Apple handles payment and purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the App reports your achievements to Apple's Game Center. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We receive no personal data from it. If you are not signed in, nothing is reported.

Children's privacy

Leviathan: Rhino is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. Because the App does not collect personal information from any user, no data is collected from children.

Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only third party involved in your use of the App is Apple, which operates the App Store and Game Center as described above.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will post the changes on this page with a new effective date.
