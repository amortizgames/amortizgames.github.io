The Shaman's Ark

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Shaman's Ark (“the App”), developed by Sergei Alekseev (“we,” “us,” or “the developer”), collects, uses, and discloses when you use the App on iOS and iPadOS.

The Shaman's Ark does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All game progress, settings, and purchase-unlock status are stored only on your device. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to identify you.

What information the App does not collect

The App does not request or access any of the following, and contains no code that references them:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your photos, camera, or microphone
  • Your contacts, calendar, or health data
  • Any device or advertising identifier used for tracking
  • Any data used for cross-app or cross-site tracking — we do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, because we do not track

We do not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or attribution software development kit (SDK). Unity's own optional analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, and telemetry systems are all disabled in this build.

What information stays on your device

The App saves your game progress, in-game settings (such as your selected language), and — if you purchase the full game — a record that the purchase was made. This information is stored in a single file inside the App's private storage area on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and we never receive a copy of it.

If you delete the App, this information is deleted along with it. If your device backs up app data (for example, via iCloud or a computer backup), that backup is controlled entirely by your own device settings, not by us.

In-app purchases

The Shaman's Ark offers one optional purchase — a one-time “Full Adventure Unlock” that permanently unlocks the complete game. All payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's In-App Purchase system. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account — Apple collects that information directly, under Apple's own privacy policy, not ours. The only thing our App stores about your purchase is a local “unlocked” flag on your own device, as described above.

For information about how Apple handles payment and purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The Shaman's Ark is rated for ages 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, there is no data collected from children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations elsewhere.

Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any information to share. The only third party involved in your use of the App is Apple, which operates the App Store and processes in-app purchases as described above, under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change — for example, if a future update adds a feature that does collect information — we will update this page and, where required, update the App's App Store privacy label and give in-app notice before the change takes effect.
