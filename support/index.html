Support

For iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 1 September 2026

Every message is read and answered by a person. Write to amortizgames@gmail.com in English or Russian, and please include the details listed below — they usually turn a long back-and-forth into a single reply.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you can send, the faster it can be fixed:

  • Your device — for example “iPhone 15 Pro” or “iPad Air (M2)”
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version — Settings → General → About → Software Version
  • The game's version — Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) → The Shaman's Ark
  • Where it happened — which area of the game, and what you were doing at the time
  • What happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or a screen recording, if the problem is something you can see

If the game crashes, it helps to say whether it happens every time or only occasionally, and whether it started after a particular point in the story.

Purchases

The Shaman's Ark is free to download and play through its opening chapters. Going further is unlocked by a single one-time purchase, the Full Adventure Unlock, offered at the stockade gate that bars the way onward. There is nothing else to buy, and the unlock is permanent.

“I paid, but the game is still locked”

This is almost always fixed from inside the game, without contacting anyone:

  • Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, and that it has an internet connection.
  • Return to the stockade gate that blocked your way. When the offer appears, choose Restore purchase instead of buying again.
  • The game will confirm on screen once the unlock arrives, and the gate opens immediately — there is no need to restart.

If you bought the unlock through Ask to Buy (Family Sharing), the purchase only completes once the family organiser approves it. Until then the gate stays shut; it opens by itself when the approval comes through.

If Restore purchase reports that nothing was found, the charge was most likely made on a different Apple Account. Apple, not us, handles all billing for App Store purchases — you can look up a charge, or report a problem with one, at reportaproblem.apple.com. If none of that resolves it, write to us with the date of the purchase and we will help you work out what happened.

Purchases are tied to the store they were made in. Owning the game on another platform does not unlock the iOS version, and vice versa.

Your saved progress

The game saves automatically as you play. Your progress, your settings and your unlock status are stored in the app's own private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to sign in to.

This has one consequence worth knowing before it bites: deleting the app deletes your progress with it, and progress does not move between devices on its own. Whether a device backup (iCloud or a computer) captures the save is governed entirely by your own backup settings, not by the game.

The purchase itself is never lost this way. Reinstalling and choosing Restore purchase always brings the unlock back, even when the save file is gone.

Languages

The game is playable in English, Russian, German, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. The language is chosen in the game's settings and can be changed at any time. If you find a translation that reads wrongly, that is a bug worth reporting — please include a screenshot and the language you were playing in.

Other platforms

The Shaman's Ark is also available on Steam, and a Nintendo Switch version is in development. This page covers the iPhone and iPad version; for questions about another platform, please say which one you are playing on when you write.
